# S13: MVMVA and GPF entered directly, and an audio meter

After S12, the heaviest seconds of level 1 at PAL60 (PPSSPP, `cpu_shift` 1) slept only 40-70 ms.
About 65k GTE commands a second still went through the door, at ~1.4 µs each, and MVMVA and GPF
were most of them.

## 1. Entries that read their word

S10's entries (`layout::GTE_OWN`) take no command word. The command they run is fixed, and words
with other bits fall back to the door. MVMVA has too many words for that: matrix, vector,
translation, sf and lm are all fields of the word. Level 1 alone uses eight of them. GPF has sf
and lm.

`layout::GTE_WORD` gives these two commands an entry each. The entry is called exactly like
`GTE_CMD`, with the word in `k1`:

```
sw ra,GPR31(k0); lui k1,hi; jal GTE_MVMVA; ori k1,k1,lo
```

| Command | Words | Entry |
|---|---|---|
| MVMVA | `w & 0x3F == 0x12` | `GTE_MVMVA` (`0x09A00FC8`) |
| GPF | `w & 0x3F == 0x3D` | `GTE_GPF` (`0x09A00FF8`) |

The routines in `trampolines.S` (`psxrt_gte_mvmva`, `psxrt_gte_gpf`) read their fields with `ext`:

- **MVMVA.** Matrix base `k0 + mx * 32`, vector V0-V2 or IR. Translation is TR or BK, or none,
  which reads `psxrt_zero3`. Each row is T << 12 in HI:LO plus three `madd`. MAC is shifted by sf
  and IR is saturated by lm.
- **GPF.** IR0 times IR1-3, then the colour FIFO pushed with saturated bytes.

Some words go on to `psxrt_gte_cmd` with everything restored:

- mx = 3 and cv = 2, the hardware's two buggy forms;
- a translation that does not fit 31 bits, for which a MAC could overflow 44 bits.

The translator's differential harness treats a `GTE_WORD` entry like `GTE_CMD`: the word is in
`k1`.

## 2. Checked at boot

`native_init`'s check now covers the eight MVMVA words of LEV01/02, `0x000CA412` (LLM, BK, lm),
and three GPF words, on 4096 random register sets each. The random registers are canonical:

- IR0-IR3 are 16-bit sign-extended, as the hardware keeps them;
- TR and BK are drawn with 31-bit magnitudes.

The first build failed the check on IR1-3 of every MVMVA and GPF word. The saturation macro had
`sra $9,$8,31` in a delay slot, and it overwrote IR even when the value was in range. The sign
now goes to `ra`. The log reads:

    native: GTE NCLIP/AVSZ/RTPS/RTPT/MVMVA/GPF in asm, 0 of 69632 checks differ

## 3. Measured

The translated code changed, so `level1.snap` was taken again: `snap.pad`, L+R+START at frame
7000. The header's code-directory hash refuses a snapshot from an older translation.

Bench, 10 s from the snapshot (PAL60, `cpu_shift` 1):

| | S12 | S13 |
|---|---|---|
| speed | 100% | 100% |
| vblanks / game fps | 59 / 58.7 | 59 / 58.7 |
| late frames | 0 | 0 |
| sleep ms/s, average | 204 | 234 |
| sleep ms/s, heaviest seconds | 40-70 | 106-111 |

The `gte ops` line no longer lists 12 or 3D. The door is left with OP (0C), DPCS (10) and SQR
(28), about 8k commands a second.

## 4. Audio meter

Each second's log line now ends its audio part with `peak in/out`:

- `in` is the largest sample the SPU wrote to the ring;
- `out` is the largest sample sent to the PSP channel after resampling.

A block from an empty ring went out silent without counting as a gap, so total silence used to
read as "gaps 0". The peaks show it now. In level 1 both sides read 5000-15000.

A bench run stops the runtime after its 12 s (`STOP: bench done`). The screen goes black and the
sound stops while PPSSPP stays open. That is the bench ending, not a fault.
