# S5: level 1 playable

## Proof

- **Input reaches the game:** the scripted pad (`native\move.pad`) presses its way to level 1
  (Start/Cross up to f 5700). It then holds right for 120 frames, up for 120, left for 60 and
  Cross for 10.
- **The level answers:**
  - The PSP shots (VRAM readback) show the character walking right into the ravine and then
    up the path, with the camera following.
  - The simulator, on the same inputs, gives the same frames at 6120 and 6320: same position,
    same camera, same scenery. The flat look of the ravine is the level's own.
  - At 6560 the two differ because the pad scripts hold Cross for 10 frames on the PSP and
    6 frames in the simulator, so the jump differs.
- **Speed in level 1 (PPSSPP):**
  - 100% at 50 fps (PAL), 190-280 ms/s of sleep.
  - Interpreter 0-3 ms/s, about 110 dispatch misses and 1-2k interpreted steps a second.

## Overlays

- Each level is a whole executable translated ahead of time. Its code (4.6 MB for LEV01) is
  read from DATA.PSAR when the guest first jumps into its text and the hash matches.
- The load costs one second at 77% speed (the blocking read), then the level runs at 100%.
- The 3.8 s/s of interpreter time noted at S4c came before the S6b DMA fix. A new
  measurement shows 2 ms in that second.

## Leaf functions behind pointers

- **The problem:** discovery found functions from the entry, calls, switch tables and
  `addiu sp,-N` prologues. A leaf without a stack frame, whose address is built at run time
  and called through a pointer, was not found.
  - Sheep Dog's pad getter at 0x8009AD24 is one: the "press X" screen ran it interpreted,
    68k misses and 495k steps a second.
- **The rule:** a word straight after a `jr ra`, its delay slot and up to 3 zero words, not yet
  reached, starts a function (`Origin::AfterReturn`). The condition: every word from there
  decodes up to its own `jr ra` (and its slot) within 64 instructions.
  - Code that follows an early return inside a function is reached by its branch first, so
    the rule does not seed it.
  - Data rarely decodes cleanly all the way to a `jr ra`.
- **Translation:**
  - 40,602 → 42,816 functions over the 26 executables; guest code covered 89.0% → 89.7%.
  - No hazards, no invalid stops. LEV00's functions all match the oracle (898 compared,
    898 identical).
- **Result:**

  | Screen | Before | After |
  |---|---|---|
  | "Press X" | 68k misses/s, 495k steps/s, interp ~700 ms/s | 67 misses/s, interp 0 ms/s |
  | Level 1 | ~500 misses/s, ~4.5k steps/s | ~110 misses/s, ~1.3k steps/s |
