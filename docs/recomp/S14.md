# S14: the polygon fast lane takes cut and empty polygons, and the first real-PSP numbers

## 1. Where the GPU time went

New counters split the polygon time. `gpu: poly n/ms (slow ms)` times the polygons that left the
fast lane. The `ge:` line now counts why they left. In the heaviest seconds of level 1 (PPSSPP,
PAL60, `cpu_shift` 1):

- the fast lane drew 61-65k polygons a second, in about 190-200 ms;
- about 9k more went the slow way, costing 47 ms:
  - ~4.5k had a vertex left of or above the VRAM origin;
  - ~4.5k had no area.

## 2. The cuts

`rd_poly` takes both kinds now.

- **No area.** A triangle with no area returns at once. The core draws nothing for it either: its
  `triangle()` returns on a zero edge function, and the texpage was already set before the fast
  lane ran.
- **Cut at the origin.** Each of the core's triangles is cut against x and y >= -0.25, as
  `polygon()` does, for a quad its 0,1,2 and 1,2,3. Each triangle becomes a fan in the ring, and
  `fl_draw` writes that fan's list words.
- **Too large.** A polygon wider than 1023 or taller than 511 is still turned away (`big`). The
  core drops those triangle by triangle.

Compare mode (`psxrt.trace` 8) checks the software rasterizer against the GE, from `level1.snap`,
over the same frames. With the cut lane, about 500 polygons a second take it, and the differing
pixels match the build without it within 0.6%: 52505 against 52204, 50922 against 50764, and
49161 against 49107. The first differing pixel is the same in each second; those are dithering
differences of the oracle.

PPSSPP bench, 10 s from the snapshot: draw 153 → 136 ms/s, dma2 164 → 149 ms/s.

## 3. The real PSP is slower than PPSSPP says

The Host ran an EBOOT on a real PSP, with no side files, so `cpu_shift` was 0:

| | PPSSPP | PSP |
|---|---|---|
| intro video (MDEC), frame ~2100 | 100%, 59 vbl, 8 fps | 28%, 16 vbl, 2 fps |
| level 1, frame ~7900 | 100%, 59 vbl, 58.9 fps | 68%, 40 vbl, 26 fps |

PPSSPP's clock counts about a cycle an instruction. It does not count cache misses, multiply and
divide latency, or uncached accesses. On the real PSP the same work costs about 1.5x (level) to
3.5x (MDEC) what PPSSPP shows.

PPSSPP stays the oracle for correctness, and for before and after comparisons. Speed is decided
from the PSP's own `psxrt.log`, which is written next to the EBOOT at each boot.
